# Privacy Policy

**wtch** does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

## What wtch does

- Fetches public status pages (e.g. GitHub, OpenAI, Cloudflare) to display service health
- Executes user-configured scripts locally on your machine
- Stores configuration locally in `~/.config/wtch/wtch.yml`
- Stores debug logs locally in `~/.config/wtch/` (only when enabled by the user)

## What wtch does NOT do

- No telemetry or analytics
- No tracking or cookies
- No data sent to third parties
- No user accounts or authentication
- No network requests other than the ones explicitly configured by the user

## Data storage

All data stays on your machine. Nothing is sent anywhere except the HTTP requests you configure to public status page APIs.

## User responsibility

wtch is a presentation layer that displays information from sources you configure. You are solely responsible for the URLs, scripts, API keys, and credentials you add to your configuration. wtch does not validate, filter, or moderate the content it fetches or the commands it executes on your behalf.

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy, open an issue at https://github.com/ysaunier/wtch/issues.
